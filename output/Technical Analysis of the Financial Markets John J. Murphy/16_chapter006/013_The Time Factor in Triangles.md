## The Time Factor in Triangles


One final factor to be considered on the subject of triangles is that of the time dimension. The triangle is considered an intermediate pattern, meaning that it usually takes longer than a month to form, but generally less than three months. A triangle that lasts less than a month is probably a different pattern, such as a pennant, which will be covered shortly. As mentioned earlier, triangles sometimes appear on long term price charts, but their basic meaning is always the same.
