## Measuring Technique for the Double Top


The measuring technique for the double top is the height of the pattern projected from the breakdown point (the point where the middle trough at point B is broken). As an alternative, measure the height of the first downleg (points A to B) and project that length downward from the middle trough at point B. Measurements at the bottom are the same, but in the other direction.
