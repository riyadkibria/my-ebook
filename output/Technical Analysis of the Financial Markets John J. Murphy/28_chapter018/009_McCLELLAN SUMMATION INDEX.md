## McCLELLAN SUMMATION INDEX


The Summation Index is simply a longer range version of the McClellan Oscillator. The McClellan Summation Index is a cumulative sum of each day’s positive or negative readings in the McClellan Oscillator. Whereas the McClellan Oscillator is used for short to intermediate trading purposes, the Summation Index provides a longer range view of market breadth and is used to spot major market turning points.
