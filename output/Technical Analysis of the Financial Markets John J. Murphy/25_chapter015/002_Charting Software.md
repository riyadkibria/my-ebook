## Charting Software


Several of the technical routines available in charting software have been covered in previous chapters. We’ll review some of the tools and indicators currently available. We’ll then address some additional features such as the ability to automate the various functions chosen by the user. In addition to providing us with the various technical studies, the computer also enables us to test various studies for profitability, which may be the most valuable feature of the program. Some software allows the user, with little or no programming background, to construct indicators and systems.
