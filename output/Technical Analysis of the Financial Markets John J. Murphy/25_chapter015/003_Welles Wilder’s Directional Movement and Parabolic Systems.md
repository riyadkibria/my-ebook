## Welles Wilder’s Directional Movement and Parabolic Systems


We’ll take a close look at a couple of Welles Wilder’s more popular systems, the Directional Movement System and the Parabolic System. We’ll use those two systems in our discussion of the relative merits of relying on mechanical trading systems. It will be demonstrated that mechanical trend following systems only work well in certain types of market environments. It will also be shown how a mechanical system can be incorporated into one’s market analysis and used simply as a confirming technical indicator.
