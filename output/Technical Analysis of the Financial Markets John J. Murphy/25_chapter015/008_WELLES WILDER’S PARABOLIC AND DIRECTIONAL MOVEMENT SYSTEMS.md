## WELLES WILDER’S PARABOLIC AND DIRECTIONAL MOVEMENT SYSTEMS


We’re going to spend some time on two studies that are especially useful. Both studies were developed by J. Welles Wilder Jr. and discussed in his book, New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems. Three of Wilder’s other studies included on the computer menu—Commodity Selection Index, Relative Strength Index, and the Swing Index—are also included in the same book.
