## THE HORIZONTAL COUNT


One principal advantage of the intraday 1 box reversal chart is the ability to obtain price objectives through use of the horizontal count. If you think back to our coverage of bar charts and price patterns, the question of price objectives was discussed. However, virtually all methods of obtaining price objectives off bar charts were based on what we call vertical measurements. This meant measuring the height of a pattern (the volatility) and projecting that distance upward or downward. For example, the head and shoulders pattern measured the distance from the head to the neckline and swung that objective from the break of that neckline.
