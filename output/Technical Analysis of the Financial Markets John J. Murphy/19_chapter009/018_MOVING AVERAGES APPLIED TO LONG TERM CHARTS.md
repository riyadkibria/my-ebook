## MOVING AVERAGES APPLIED TO LONG TERM CHARTS


The reader should not overlook using this technique in longer range trend analysis. Longer range moving averages, such as 10 or 13 weeks, in conjunction with the 30 or 40 week average, have long been used in stock market analysis, but haven’t been given as much attention in the futures markets. The 10 and 40 week moving averages can be used to help track the primary trend on weekly charts for futures and stocks. (See Figure 9.10.)
