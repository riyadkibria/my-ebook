## REFERENCE MATERIAL


Two of the best sources of information on Elliott Wave Theory and the Fibonacci numbers are The Major Works of R.N. Elliott, (Prechter, Jr.) and the Elliott Wave Principle (Frost and Prechter). All of the diagrams used in Figures 13.1-13.24 are from the Elliott Wave Principle and are reproduced in this chapter through the courtesy of New Classics Library.

A primer booklet on the Fibonacci numbers, Understanding Fibonacci Numbers by Edward D. Dobson, is available from Traders Press (P.O. Box 6206, Greenville, S.C. 29606 (800-927-8222).
