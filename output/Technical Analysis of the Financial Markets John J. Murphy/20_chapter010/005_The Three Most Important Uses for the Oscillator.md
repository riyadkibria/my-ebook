## The Three Most Important Uses for the Oscillator


There are three situations when the oscillator is most useful. You’ll see that these three situations are common to most types of oscillators that are used.

Figure 10.1a The 10 day momentum line fluctuates around a zero line. Readings too far above the zero line are overbought, while values too far below the line are oversold. Momentum should be used in conjunction with the trend of the market.
