## LARRY WILLIAMS %R


Larry Williams %R is based on a similar concept of measuring the latest close in relation to its price range over a given number of days. Today’s close is subtracted from the price high of the range for a given number of days and that difference is divided by the total range for the same period. The concepts already discussed for oscillator interpretation are applied to %R as well, with the main factors being the presence of divergences in overbought or oversold areas. (See Figure 10.18.) Since %R is subtracted from the high, it looks like an upside down stochastics. To correct that, charting packages plot an inverted version of %R.

Figure 10.18 Larry Williams %R oscillator is used in the same fashion as other oscillators. Readings over 80 or under 20 identify market extremes.
