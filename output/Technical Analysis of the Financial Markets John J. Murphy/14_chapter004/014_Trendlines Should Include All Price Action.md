## Trendlines Should Include All Price Action


Trendlines on bar charts should be drawn over or under the entire day’s price range. Some chartists prefer to draw the trendline by connecting only the closing prices, but that is not the more standard procedure. The closing price may very well be the most important price of the day, but it still represents only a small sample of that day’s activity. The technique of including the day’s price range takes into account all of the activity and is the more common usage. (See Figure 4.8.)
