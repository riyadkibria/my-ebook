## Classification of Cycles


The general categories are: long term cycles (2 or more years in length), the seasonal cycle (1 year), the primary or intermediate cycle (9 to 26 weeks), and the trading cycle (4 weeks). The trading cycle breaks down into two shorter alpha and beta cycles, which average 2 weeks each. (The labels Primary, Trading, Alpha, and Beta are used by Walt Bressert to describe the various cycle lengths.) (See Figure 14.13.)
