About the Author ABOUT THE AUTHOR © MARIANNE KILROY CHRISTOPHER LEONARD is a reporter whose work has appeared in the New York Times, Bloomberg Businessweek, Time , and elsewhere. He is the author of The Meat Racket and the New York Times bestseller Kochland , which won the J. Anthony Lukas Work-in-Progress Award. He is director of the Watchdog Writers Group, an investigative reporting program at the University of Missouri School of Journalism.
